# G5 statement-contract routing delivery

Saved individual questions now support statement review bound to the original source and existing owned work. The user selects an exact source passage and an explicit existing local role. Agere checks all 24 required statement, time and care-relation labels before inference. The lexical and windowed MiniLM heads cannot express eight of them, so this path records an explicit abstention, with the missing labels visible. Existing shadow comparisons remain available separately. A Kev/d1 proposal stays experimental and can open the existing interpretation-review controls; it does not become an accepted interpretation or change care work automatically.

The implementation uses the existing evidence dialog, question decision and follow-up storage. It verifies the patient, selected record hash, task/plan/option binding and current task state. Exact replay is idempotent; reopening checks source, task, model, implementation and linked reading dependencies. Changed dependencies or an attempted weaker contract are rejected. Individual patient review is not silently generalized to population decisions.

## Supervision feasibility and decision

The registered cohort has 306 machine-teacher targets: 209 fit, 49 calibration and 48 validation. All eight unsupported labels are absent from every partition: statement `other_person` and `retracted`; time `planned` and `unknown`; relation `administration`, `care_completion`, `record_request` and `source_coverage`.

A predeclared offline audit counted 1,238,686 received records and inspected 197,940 eligible received narrative fields after excluding protected evaluation families, nontraining partitions and the four diagnosed patient families. Lexical cues identify possible further development sources, not confirmed labels. Planned-time cues mostly belong to components with conflicting inherited partitions; unknown-time cues all do. Source-coverage cues provide no disjoint full fit/calibration/evaluation support. No retraction or care-completion cue matches were found by these limited patterns; this does not prove those meanings are absent from the warehouse. Inspected examples are recorded as development exposure and cannot later be called untouched evaluation.

This audit did not establish a defensible full-contract, family-disjoint supervised experiment. No new fit, relabeling, partition repair or manufactured tiny evaluation was performed. The classifier contract remains 16/24 reachable labels. Explicit abstention closes a workflow failure; it is not improved semantic accuracy or model qualification.
