    A QUICK TOUR

    One question. Many possible next steps.

    1. Ask a complete question. Include who or what, the measure, and the period you care about. You can ask beyond the example cards.
    2. Explore the result. Switch between the overview, returned data, and method. Missing information stays visible.
    3. Keep the scope clear. “Follow up on this result” keeps the saved patient set. Choose “Entire warehouse” for a new analysis.
    4. Open the action workspace. Inspect the cohort, prepare review work, or make a command-center mockup. Actions are simulated.
