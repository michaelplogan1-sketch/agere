### utilization_event(patient_id, kind, event_date, end_date, discharge_known, sources, in_ehr, source_records, record_id, organization_id, readmitted_30d, days_to_readmission)
All-source utilization: one row per ED visit (kind ed_visit) or hospital admission (inpatient | observation) per patient, merging EHR encounters/stays, ADT visits and payer claims (ED = POS 23 professional or revenue 045x/0981; inpatient = TOB 011x; observation = revenue 0762) that fall within one day of each other; sources = ehr,adt,claim; in_ehr = 1 when an EHR encounter/stay exists. Claims count only when counts_in_metrics = 1. Events not in the EHR are also rows of encounter (source_table adt_messages | payer_medical_claims, type_concept emergency_visit) and stay (stay_source adt | payer_claim), so ED and admission rates cover claims-only members.
