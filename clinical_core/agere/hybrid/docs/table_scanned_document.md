### scanned_document(record_id, patient_id, document_id, document_type, document_title, received_via, event_date, available_date, page_count, has_text_layer, review_status, signature_status, sending_facility, file_status, text_source, readable, unreadable_reason, text_chars, records_sharing_file, pdf_date_of_service, duplicate_of_record_id)
Outside documents (faxes, scans, portal uploads) with indexing as received (document_type is often a generic label), review/signature status, and readability: text_source = pdf_text_layer | front_office_ocr (OCR text with errors); readable = 0 with unreadable_reason = image_only_no_ocr | file_missing | file_belongs_to_another_record | file_unverified_shared. duplicate_of_record_id = same patient, title, date and text as an earlier fax.
