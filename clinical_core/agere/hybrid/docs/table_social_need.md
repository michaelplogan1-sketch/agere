### social_need(patient_id, need, positive, instrument, source_stream, source_field, event_date, record_id)
Social-needs screening answers normalised across instruments: one row per (screen record, need) with positive 0/1. need = food | transport | cost_of_care, plus housing | utilities | safety from AHC HRSN screens (instrument AHC_HRSN; positive when any item for the need is answered yes; source_field = items:<item names>). transport positive = transport_difficulty_reported or transport unreliable (family_ride/reliable = negative); cost_of_care = difficulty paying for care (social screen) or medical bills (financial screen). Screened = has a row for that need.
