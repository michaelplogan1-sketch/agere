### note_fact(patient_id, record_id, stream, fact_type, value, unit, negated, temporality, event_date, evidence_text, extractor, confidence, qualifier, experiencer, source_field, span_start, span_end)
Facts found only in free text (EF, symptoms, edema, dyspnea, medication non-adherence and reasons, social needs, imaging findings, follow-up instructions, goals of care, smoking status) in notes, documents and HIE ED/discharge summaries, with negation, temporality and evidence spans; copied-forward note lines count once per patient; rule extractor admitted per fact type at >= 0.90 measured precision.
