### medication(record_id, patient_id, source_stream, evidence, drug_name, drug_class, status, event_date, available_date, ingredients, therapeutic_category, rxnorm, rxnorm_source, rxnorm_ingredient, ndc, fill_reversed, picked_up)
Every medication mention, one row per (record, drug_class): source_stream and evidence (dispensed, claim, ordered, listed, administered, reported, pharmacy_history, payer_claim = payer pharmacy claim), drug_name as received, status. drug_class from the active ingredient (statin, beta_blocker, hf_evidence_based_beta_blocker, ace_inhibitor, arb, arni, mra, sglt2_inhibitor, glp1_agonist, metformin, insulin, anticoagulant, opioid, ...); NULL when unclassified. drug_class values are exact ids (full list and groups in drug_class_vocabulary): compare with = or IN, never LIKE - '%beta%' also matches short_acting_beta_agonist and '%antihistamine%' matches both generations. Use beta_blocker, first_generation_antihistamine (Beers), second_generation_antihistamine, short_acting_beta_agonist. picked_up = 1 on fills that left the pharmacy (evidence dispensed | claim | payer_claim with status dispensed | paid | completed, not reversed / returned to stock; prepared-only dispenses never count): use picked_up = 1 for any fill, pickup or dispensing count. ONE pickup can arrive from two sources: the dispensing pharmacy (pharmacy_dispenses, days in payload days_supply_reported) and the payer's pharmacy claim (payer_pharmacy_claims / pharmacy_claims, days in payload days_supply). For fill counts or days' supply never add the sources together: take one fill per patient, drug class and fill date (e.g. per patient+event_date the larger days supply of the two sources), then sum over dates; counting patients needs no such step.
