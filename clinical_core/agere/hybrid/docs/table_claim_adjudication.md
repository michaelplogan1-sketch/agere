### claim_adjudication(claim_id, claim_source, record_id, patient_id, sequence, status, frequency_code, event_date, service_date, payer, billed_cents, payer_paid_delta_cents, allowed_delta_cents, replaces_claim_id)
One row per adjudication of a medical claim from either source (claim_source medical_claims = our provider remittances, payer_medical_claims = the payer feed). payer_paid_delta_cents / allowed_delta_cents: reversals negative, recoupments negative paid and 0 allowed, a void reverses the prior net; facility DRG rows without deltas use their paid/allowed amounts. status paid | denied | reversed | voided | recouped ...; replaces_claim_id = the original claim of a frequency-7 replacement. Net paid in a period = sum of deltas by event_date; claims reversed by the payer = distinct claim_id with status reversed.
