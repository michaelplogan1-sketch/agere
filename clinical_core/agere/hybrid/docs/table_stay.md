### stay(patient_id, admit_date, discharge_date, type, organization_id, readmitted_30d, days_to_readmission, discharge_known, discharge_date_source, discharge_time_utc, admit_time_utc, los_days, los_days_elapsed, stay_source)
Distinct hospital stays (inpatient/observation encounters collapsed by admit day and overlap) with 30-day readmission flag. discharge_date and los_days are NULL when no discharge time was received (discharge_known = 0); never treat those as 0 days. discharge_date is the ADT discharge (A03) date of the stay's encounter when one was received (discharge_date_source = adt), else the date part of the encounter end_time (encounter_end_time_utc); discharge_time_utc keeps the UTC end timestamp. Readmission gaps use discharge_date. LOS uses one clock: admit_time_utc = UTC start of the admitting encounter; los_days = midnights between the UTC dates of admit_time_utc and discharge_time_utc, los_days_elapsed = fractional days between the two timestamps (state which one an answer uses); ADT/claims-only stays count local admit to local discharge dates. stay_source = encounters, or adt | payer_claim for admissions found only in ADT visits or inpatient (TOB 011x) / observation (revenue 0762) payer claims, so admission rates cover claims-only members; readmission flags of a patient with such stays are recomputed over all of the patient's stays.
