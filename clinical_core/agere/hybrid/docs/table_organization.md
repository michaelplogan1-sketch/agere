### organization(organization_id, organization_type, display_name, npi, taxonomy_code, tin, city, state, postal_code, facility_class, place_of_service, place_of_service_claims)
Organization reference rows: organization_type, npi, taxonomy_code, tin, city/state/postal_code; facility_class (hospital, ambulatory_surgery_center, imaging_idtf, urgent_care, dialysis, skilled_nursing, home_health, hospice, ambulance, dme, pharmacy, laboratory, primary_care, outpatient_practice, payer, other) from the directory type; place_of_service = most frequent CMS POS on claims billed under the organization NPI (place_of_service_claims = n).
