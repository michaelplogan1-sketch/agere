### enrollment_span(record_id, patient_id, member_id, payer_id, product, line_of_business, plan_id, coverage_start, coverage_end, enrollment_reason, termination_reason, dual_status, attribution_practice_id, attribution_npi, available_date, coverage_end_received, open_ended, previous_member_id, member_id_reissued)
One row per received payer enrollment span: payer_id, product (commercial, marketplace, medicare_ffs, medicare_advantage, medicaid, dual_ffs, dual_ma), line_of_business (commercial | medicare | medicaid | dual), plan_id, coverage_start/coverage_end (NULL end = open), termination_reason, payer attribution (attribution_practice_id/attribution_npi).
