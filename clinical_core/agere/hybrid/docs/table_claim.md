### claim(claim_id, patient_id, payer, service_date, benefit_year, first_status, final_status, ever_denied, denied_then_paid, net_payer_paid_cents, billed_cents, allowed_cents, first_available_date, last_available_date, service_organization_id, versions, first_submitted_date, first_payer_cash_date, net_payer_cash_cents, first_adjudication_date, received_final_status, live_payer_paid_cents, drg, drg_grouper_version, principal_diagnosis, diagnosis_codes, billing_npi, rendering_npi, place_of_service, type_of_bill, revenue_code, service_code_system, service_code, local_charge_code, admission_date, discharge_date, cpt, hcpcs, claim_source, first_paid_date, first_reversal_date)
One row per medical claim_id (adjudications arrive as successive records: denied, paid, reversed, re-paid). first_status by arrival order; final_status follows the adjudication chain (each reversal paired with the payment it reverses; final = last unreversed adjudication by event time), received_final_status = last-arrival status, live_payer_paid_cents = payer paid on unreversed payments; denied_then_paid = a denial later followed by payment; net_payer_paid_cents = paid minus reversed. Legacy rows without claim_id are excluded. claim_source = medical_claims (our provider remittances) | payer_medical_claims (payer-feed claims that are not the payer copy of one of our claims; a -R replacement folds into its original): every claim of the population. first_paid_date / first_reversal_date per claim; per-adjudication money in claim_adjudication.
