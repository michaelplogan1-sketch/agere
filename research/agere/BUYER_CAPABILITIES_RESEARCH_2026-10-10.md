# Buyer services and patient pathways — research intake, October 10, 2026

The user requires Agere to know what each buyer can deliver, understand patient-specific CMS and commercial pathways, and translate supported needs into work for clinicians, agents and teams. Pharmacy, HF, pulmonary rehabilitation, palliative care and virtual PCP are examples, not a scope boundary. The delivered first implementation is described in `BUYER_CAPABILITIES_BUILD.md`.

Primary-source webpages were checked with web browsing on October 10. This is discovery-level review, not exhaustive rule extraction, full contract review, clinical validation or verified participation. The runtime catalog contains brief original summaries, source links, the checked date and unresolved prerequisites. Public pages can change. The active synthetic warehouse has a 2029 cutoff; it is not valid to silently apply the 2026 policy review as verified 2029 policy.

The 22 service templates also cover buyer-specific affordability assistance, transportation, food/meal support and discharge follow-through. They are product configuration examples, not claims that these services are available to a particular patient. A buyer can enter custom tags and services without a software change. No templates are silently installed as available buyer services.
