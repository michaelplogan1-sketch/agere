## Verification and corrections

- **#1:** [REVOIR](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37588), §§2–4 and conclusion,
  uses weighted LLM-generated intent hypotheses, simulated replies and an internal
  reward proxy. Its one-step lookahead is myopic; it does not establish Agere's
  probabilities, interruption costs, interleaved execution or clinical utility.
  The [timing study](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07937), §§3–6, measures forced
  information injection, not a deployed ask policy. Detailed MCP-Atlas goal
  pass@3 is .78/.50/.44/.39/.39 at 10/30/50/70/90%, against .40 without clarification:
  “loses nearly all value after 10%” overstates the result. The 52% over-asking
  figure describes GPT-5.2, not all 300 sessions. [SAGE](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08798),
  §§4–8, uses structured tool/argument candidates and a perfect-resolution proxy;
  [VoI communication](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.06407), §§4–6 and limitations,
  uses predefined questions/actions and simplified communication costs. Adopt the
  decision principle, not reported numerical gains or assumed user reliability.
- **#2:** [JevAgentHarness](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21412), §§2.1,3–7,
  Appendix A and limitations, distinguishes isolated, recorded-state and executed
  task evaluation. Shadow trajectories estimate confidence reliability, incumbent
  correctness, call price and the downstream cost of a wrong choice; thresholds
  are per decision and refitted on changes. The quoted 14.2% result is a particular
  500-task experiment. The combined Jev delegation failed non-inferiority and
  telecom transfer lost 8.1 points. Do not copy .5, .99 or 1.00 into Agere.
- **#3:** [HarnessMM](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32813), §§3–6 and Appendix D,
  supports literal actions, dependency closure and guard-based deferral. Its
  HumanEval numbers refer to 98 test tasks and specific harness configurations;
  guards establish declared public conditions, not semantic correctness.
- **#4:** [EvoOntology](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15779), Method, Experiments
  and Analyses, grounds typed edits in trajectories and gates them on a
  backbone-conditional validation comparison. This supports controlled evolution,
  not self-approval of invented program rules or reuse of reserved evaluations.
  The current paper is internally inconsistent about backbone count: the abstract
  says four, while Experimental Setup lists six. The sweep's four is therefore
  not a reliable description of the complete current experiments.
- **#5:** [EHR-Complex](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.23301), §§3–4 and limitations,
  uses credentialed MIMIC-IV and sandboxed computation; synthetic healthcare here
  is a different distribution. [DataSpace](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.03451),
  §§4.5,6 and evaluation appendix, checks complete typed tables with declared
  ordering/precision. The 15.36-point spread compares different **agent harnesses**
  at fixed backbone, not changing the evaluator alone. Header-invariant matching
  must not silently swap semantically different measures, units or denominators.
- **#6:** [Role-stratified CRC](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24343), Method and
  Experiments, calibrates semantic argument roles in prompt-injection/tool-call
  experiments. [CORA](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09155), framework and experiments,
  concerns mobile GUI guardians and execution abstention. Their calibration and
  distribution assumptions do not confer clinical or financial safety here.
- **#7:** [Information Set Emulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17777),
  certificate construction and simulation sections, explicitly uses theory and
  simulations without patient data. AIPW remains conditional on identification,
  timing and nuisance-estimation assumptions. The related
  [MNAR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05125), methods/evaluation/discussion,
  does not justify recovering arbitrary missing facts from language.
- **#8:** [Always-On Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30306), §§2–4 and
  governance/evaluation sections, is a survey and evaluation proposal. Its six
  axes are a useful review vocabulary, not a measured fix for this controller.
- **A:** [Sheaf Laplacian](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19529), §§4–7,
  provides the construction and energy identity; it is not an Agere empirical
  source-fusion result. [Preference conflicts](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02416),
  §§3–5, localizes incompatibility in discrete preference examples; it cannot
  simply be substituted by a linear Laplacian. The
  [sensor paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.01446), §§3–5, makes the observation
  spaces/restrictions and compatibility assumptions explicit. Do not average
  away incompatible source meanings or assert that the spectrum alone locates truth.
- **B:** The downloaded [Algebraic Databases](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.03501)
  HTML exposes its introduction, so it is background rather than a completed
  methods review. [Algebraic Data Integration](https://arxiv.org/html/1503.03571),
  §§4–6, support typed equational mappings and migration under stated conditions.
  [Robot plan transfer](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.15961), approach and discussion,
  explicitly warns that missing attributes/default placeholders can invalidate a
  transferred plan. Formal structure does not prove industry meaning. Reviewed
  [self-demonstration mapping](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13776), §§3–5,
  [metadata reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07946), §§3–6, and
  [telemetry induction](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19615), §§3–4, as candidate
  proposal mechanisms, not semantic certificates.
- **C:** 2606.02994's actual title is
  [Learning Reusable Agent Subroutines from Experience](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02994),
  not the title in the sweep. §§3–6 show callable natural-language/LLM subroutines;
  this is not evidence that compiling arbitrary traces to native code helps.
  [Prospective Compression](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09985) studies a
  nonstationary abstraction task; [LILO](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.19791),
  §§2–4, combines synthesis, compression and documentation in bounded DSLs.
- **D:** [Csql](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08109), §§3–4,12–13, stores extracted
  causal structures, uncertainty and provenance for deterministic querying.
  This is a hypothesis/explanation scaffold, not identification of real effects.
- **E:** Reviewed [Speculate with Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12236),
  §§2–4; [cost-aware speculation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07846), decision rule,
  validation and synthetic appendix; and [Speculative Actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04371),
  §§2–4. Lossless commitment requires matching/verification, and duplicate writes
  or arbitrary predicted user replies are not authorized. The exact clarification
  branch optimization has not been demonstrated by this lane in Agere.
- **F:** [ReClaim](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02740), Results, Methods and
  Discussion, uses longitudinal MarketScan claims, post-training and healthcare
  validation. The cited AUC comparison has its stated healthcare scope. It is no
  empirical evidence for a default model across customers, machines or industries.

The proposed A+B+C+#1+#3 combination is our untested research hypothesis. No claim
that nobody has made it follows from this targeted review. Each borrowed method
must earn its complexity against native queries, explicit conflict rules, current
guarded reuse and existing operators at matched scope.
