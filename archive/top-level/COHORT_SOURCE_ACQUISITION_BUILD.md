# Broader source acquisition and verified record availability — October 10, 2026

Agere can now acquire open-vocabulary source phrases from the previously unmarked cohort and open them as exact source-learning tasks in the real `/learn` interface. It also supplies verified received-record availability to the question-linked phrase specialist, keeping unsubmitted content distinct from unreceived evidence. These are executable acquisition capabilities, not clinical validation or a newly qualified model.

## What executed

The existing 500-field cohort remains immutable. Its 125 unmarked fields span 76 categories. A separate acquisition plan assigns **118 eligible fields across 74 categories** to **65 fitting / 22 calibration / 31 development-validation fields** before inference. Seven fields with conflicting inherited components are excluded before any provider call. Original-person eligibility and existing patient/source/template/input connections are retained; protected and outer evaluation families never enter this acquisition.

All 118 eligible requests completed: **117 produced 230 exact proposals (229 distinct concept strings), one abstained, zero failed**. Proposals span all 74 eligible source categories and retain 117 fitting / 42 calibration / 71 development-validation assignments. These counts describe sampled field acquisition, not population prevalence, detection recall or semantic accuracy. The one abstention was a nonspecific `Continue management` plan fragment. Exclusions and abstention remain in the 125-field denominator.

Each 3,552–10,917-byte request contains one complete received text field, reported structured siblings, source provenance, omitted-text-field names and verified availability metadata. It contains neither a whole chart nor generator truth, review labels or partition features. The contexts include 75 resolved typed references. Other received contents remain explicitly unsubmitted and uninterpreted. Availability is bounded to the visible same-patient snapshot; missing references do not establish absent care.

Quotes and overlapping occurrence offsets are verified against the exact field. Concepts and explanatory prose remain machine proposals. Selecting a proposal creates an immutable source task; it does not change the frozen cohort, mark a human review, resolve a question or add a training label. Existing local/specialist readers are available from that task. Exact saved acquisition and selection replay incur no new model call.

Two source tasks were selected in verification: a billing-authorization account and a functional-mobility statement. The latter ran through installed local Kev and back into the saved task, with exact replay. Its labels were `patient_affirmed / current_at_source / unknown relation`, with low confidences, 850 input tokens, 10.31 seconds inference and 27.08 seconds model load. It remains unreviewed and unqualified. In particular, “homebound status not established” does not establish that the patient is not homebound or determine coverage.
