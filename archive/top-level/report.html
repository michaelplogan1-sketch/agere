A local System One model selects concepts and assertion labels. A local LLM handles question interpretation and evidence summaries. A reclassification service reruns affected records after terminology, model, or reviewer changes. SQL continues to calculate the cohort.

Current limits: eight authored notes, narrow sentence templates, a two-node demo ontology, one note per patient, one lab type. No real clinical validation or full OMOP/FHIR implementation.
