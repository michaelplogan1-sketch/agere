A saved option comparison can now decompose an intervention into its delivery components and accept an explicit service proposal for each. Combined CBT/medication review has therapy and prescribing/monitoring components. Monotherapies retain their own component. This is operational decomposition, not a clinical protocol or a treatment choice.

Distinct services can supply separate proposals; the same declared service can be proposed for both only explicitly. Having proposals for every component is distinct from verified delivery capability. Generic service names or tags do not prove that the service provides CBT or prescribing.
