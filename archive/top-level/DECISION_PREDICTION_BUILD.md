# Prediction readiness in the saved population question

The original respiratory and behavioral population decisions now execute a dated
prediction-readiness follow-up and retain its exact member sources and owned work.
This closes a shared-path wiring gap. It does **not** qualify a predictor or claim
clinical accuracy. The finite local prediction engineering requirement is 4/4;
full release engineering is now **3/9**. G5 remains active and G3 remains blocked
at 5/6 individual workflows.

The existing outcome, baseline, receipt-landmark and prediction-registry engines
are reused in `question_decision.py`, its existing API namespace, browser view and
immutable follow-up store. No new database, model or fitting pipeline was added.
Five registered index-day/receipt-day definitions are selectable. The original
population and receipt cutoff stay fixed; explicit index/receipt dates define the
follow-up question. Every selected member is accounted for, including no index,
unknown dates, late arrivals, unavailable baseline and incomplete follow-up.

Saved model reports apply only under an exact task, parameter, profile and full
evidence identity match. Their protected development-build denominator remains
separate from a selected cohort. Different windows or an unavailable/stale registry
produce an explicit unavailable comparison. No private source-family assignments,
hidden truth or development predictions become patient evidence. Frozen targets,
weights, fitting/calibration/validation partitions and activation decisions are
unchanged; no experiment was rescored or refitted.
