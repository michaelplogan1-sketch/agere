## Decisions in force (Mike), all in `clinical_core/agere/clinical/decision_context.py` (VERSION decision-context/2)

- **Patients:**
  - **Who counts:** living people. No death row, and a duplicate chart counts once (`EXCLUDE_DUPLICATE_CHARTS`, on by default). The
    deceased count only when the question asks.
  - **Age:** from the birth date, never `age_latest`.
- **Labs:** the latest result by default. "Ever", "any", "history" or "any \<lab\>" mean any result.
- **Conditions:**
  - **Evidence:** EHR diagnosis, problem list or counted claim, matched by code set, whatever the concept label.
  - **Diabetes:** also any current HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %.
  - **CKD:** also two eGFR results < 60 taken at least 90 days apart.
- **Medicare:** includes dual and Medigap. "Traditional", "original" or "fee-for-service" means coverage_type medicare only.
- **Medication use:** "on" a medication means a picked-up fill in the last 12 months, for every drug class.
- **Time windows:** "last year" is the 12 months ending at as-of.
- **Insight thresholds:**
  - frequent ED user: 3 or more visits;
  - high-cost claimant: $50k or more.
- **Models:**
  - **Question path:** Haiku first. Sonnet runs only after every Haiku attempt failed (`model_policy.ladder`, the escalation rung).
  - **Opus:** only for deliverables.
  - **Cost:** System One cost is not a constraint.

## How a question flows now (`proposers.ORDER`)

1. **Reuse and certified answers.** Saved plans replay only under the same decision-context version.
2. **`chart_reader`, for one named patient.** It covers every structured source, and notes through the Jev note frames (`note_frames`,
   `note_open`, calibration in `config/agere/note_frames_calibration.json`).
3. **The grammar and the free population, claims and insight forms** (`population_language`, `claims_language`, `population_engines`,
   `insight_families`). Exact answers, about 0.6 s, $0.
4. **`spec_path`:** grammar, System One slots and a Haiku spec writer, then verification part by part.
5. **System One routers.**
6. **The Claude stage, Haiku first:**
   - `semantic_analyst`, now with definition views, arithmetic done in code and a SQL audit (`analyst_definitions.py`);
   - the other free-form writers, whose answers need System One confirmation.

## Gotchas learned 10-23 / 10-24

When live results are far below in-process results, check these first:
- stale caches (saved plans and graph results; both are now versioned, and code_identity covers the chart and note modules);
- graph validation dropping undeclared parameters;
- contextvars lost in worker threads;
- the literal check and the guard reading patient ids or dates as numbers or terms;
- the dev runner's chaining (fixed).

In zsh, an unquoted `$VAR` holding a list does not word-split; use `tools/runtests_diff.py` (branch-vs-main failure diff) instead.
