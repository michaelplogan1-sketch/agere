# Component value and company comparison — October 27, 2026

Agere has reusable analytical, evidence-reading and artifact capabilities. We have
not established that its automatic product is clinically reliable across the
charter, optimal in cost, or better than another company's product. The accepted
assisted Heartline delivery remains physician-accepted within its recorded scope.
Its acceptance does not qualify every subsequent model, workflow or release.

## What must earn a place

| Component | Intended contribution | Evidence required beyond invocation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FTS, MiniLM, BGE and optional Qwen reranking | Recover relevant source evidence efficiently | Paired evidence recall, missed exceptions, complete-answer accuracy and total cost versus lexical/dense baselines; same population/date scope and actual model identity |
| UltraBERT catalog/terminology matching | Resolve source concepts and fields | Correct mappings and downstream answers versus simpler matching, including ambiguous/unknown cases |
| Typed/SQL local SLM adapters | Produce executable plans locally | Source-checked answer gains, losses, wrong answers and declines on separate source families; full-path cost including repair/review; no default promotion from training loss |
| Jev/Perplexity typed decisions | Review interpretation, omissions and support | Correct approval/rejection of complete evidence, with false approvals and cost measured; a real quote alone is insufficient |
| Subscription Claude readers/investigator/composer | Resolve complex narrative questions and create useful artifacts | Ordinary automatic workflow completion with full notes, patient-specific uncertainty, physician-acceptable answers and faithful delivered content |
| Native SQL and mathematical engines | Calculate exact counts, dollars, rates and supported estimates | Shared source/event/measurement eligibility before fitting; explicit denominator, uncertainty, observation versus prediction/causality, and useful advantage over appropriate baselines |
| Recursive execution reuse | Reuse verified work while rechecking evidence and outcomes | Identical scope/source/definition binding, native replay validation, fresh final review, complete downstream work and full-request API accounting; later adverse feedback retires reuse |

The current typed/SQL learning studies are finite local adapter experiments.
Their authored task-bank results are engineering measurements, not physician
scores. Preserve negative candidates, separate calibration from final comparison,
and never fit corrections from inspected comparison answers. Large question
counts and lower loss do not establish better clinical judgment. The learned
shape head's absence of useful gains beyond native rules remains a negative
result, not a reason to hide the native baseline.

## How to measure a useful advantage

Use fresh clinician/analyst-authored workflows with independently adjudicated
acceptable answers: percentages, dollars, counts and yes/no/unknown conclusions.
Cover clinical state, utilization, provider/access/social questions and financial
VBC work, including unfamiliar combinations. Keep source families, definitions,
cutoffs and final comparisons fixed. Include absent data, contradictions, empty
cohorts, late corrections and source-supported unknowns. Do not use rejected
dev3/held-out keys or tune on final comparison answers.

Run two distinct comparisons. Internal component ablations establish whether a
reranker, reader, SLM or mathematical method improves its role versus removal or
a simpler substitute, including interactions and outages. Company-product
comparisons evaluate complete customer workflows on equivalent received data,
definitions and permitted tools. A strong frontier model with the same tools is
a useful additional baseline; it is not a substitute for comparing the companies.

Report paired gains/losses, correct completed workflows, confident errors, useful
coverage, appropriate unknowns, artifact fidelity, operator effort and latency.
Separate cold and warm reuse. Cost per correct completed workflow is the cost
of **all attempts divided by correct complete workflows**. Include routing,
retrieval, local compute, readers, reviews, repairs, artifacts and amortized
training/development where measured. Separate billed subscription cost, API
equivalent estimates and unpriced work. API calls of zero do not mean zero total
cost. Until those measurements exist, cost per clinically correct Agere answer
and any company-product lead remain unmeasured.
