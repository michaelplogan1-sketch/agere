## 2. What the stack is (as built; Mike asked for no re-architecture)

- **Deterministic engines and governed metrics compute.** These are the compositional grammar, the population engines and 67 versioned
  metrics in `request_vocabulary.definitions()`, all under `decision_context.py` (decision-context/3).
- **System One (Jev, then the Perplexity decider) judges closed questions.** It routes, checks whether an answer fits the question
  (`system_one_gate.check`), re-reads cited documents for yes/no chart answers (`Cascade.system_one_disagrees`), and judges whole
  documents per question (`backstop_agent.judge_documents`).
- **Haiku writes typed specs** on the spec path, and a compiler executes them.
- **Sonnet is the investigator** (`clinical_core/agere/clinical/backstop_agent.py`). It works with tools: `list_metrics` /
  `execute_metric`, `ask_agere` (one sub-question through the free governed stages), read-only SQL with a `cohort` view, and document
  list / search / read / judge. It sees the conversation.
  - Quotes are verified against the cited record's own text or rows, and a repair may never flip negation.
  - Its answers pass `answer_checks.gate`, or a modality check. Grounding and searched-absence checks are provenance only, so those
    answers ship `unverified` / `partial`.
- **Cascade order** (`proposers.py`):
  - the investigator runs before the generalist analysts, whatever the learned routers say;
  - one-patient questions reach it right after the fast exact tiers and the chart reader;
  - a population answer that System One judges partial or a different question gets a second look from the investigator, fast paths
    included (`second_look`, `second_look_fast_path`).
- Opus is used for deliverables only.
