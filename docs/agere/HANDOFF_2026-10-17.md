## Clinical task definitions to align to (generator, 2026-10-17; published after Mike runs the regeneration)
- **Tasks and steps:**
  - CT-01: a = HFrEF set; b = missing_pillars per patient.
  - CT-03: a = rows of patient_id, drug, daily_dose_mg, target_daily_dose_mg.
  - CT-04: a = eligible; b = on neither an SGLT2i nor a GLP-1 RA.
  - CT-05: a = eligible, with cha2ds2_vasc; b = no OAC.
  - CT-06: a = eligible; b = no ACEi/ARB/ARNI; c = no SGLT2i.
  - CT-07: a = indicated; b = no statin.
  - CT-09 is deferred to v2.13.
- **Index date 2028-12-31, not as_of.** Ignore later data (deep-chart dispensing drops in 2029). Our saved CT answers
  used 2030-05-27 and must be re-run.
- **"On" a drug:** a picked-up fill (paid payer claim not reversed or recouped, current version; or a dispense whose
  latest status is dispensed) dated on or before the index date, with fill date + days_supply on or after index − 90.
  Drugs are matched by the name terms in the definitions. Metoprolol counts only as succinate/ER/XL/24 HR; ARNI is
  matched before valsartan.
- **Daily dose:** from the latest fill that states one. Claims: name mg × quantity / days_supply. Dispenses: mg ×
  label_dose × frequency. ARNI = sacubitril + valsartan mg.
- **Targets (mg/day):** carvedilol 50, metoprolol succinate 200, bisoprolol 10, ARNI 400, lisinopril 20, enalapril 20,
  ramipril 10, captopril 150, losartan 150, valsartan 320, candesartan 32, spironolactone 25, eplerenone 50,
  dapagliflozin 10, empagliflozin 10. One current drug per pillar (the most recently filled).
- **HFrEF:** I50 plus an I50.2x/I50.4x code, or a latest delivered LVEF ≤ 40% (a range counts as its lower value).
- **Labs:** latest by specimen date; a corrected result supersedes; ties take the highest. UACR LOINC 9318-7/14959-1
  (mg/g). LDL ≥ 190 = any result on or before the index date.
- **Submission format:** `{"id":"CT-01","steps":{"a":[{"patient_id":...}],"b":[{"patient_id":...,"missing_pillars":[...]}]}}`.
- **Scoring:** `python3 -m ecosystem.eval_scorer --release releases/healthcare-warehouse-v2.8 --clinical-tasks <file>
  --sample-release <v2.8 sample dir>`. Doses must match within 0.01 mg; the score must match exactly.
