# Agere audit, 2026-10-25 (evening): why an LLM-backed stack still missed questions, and what changed

Requested by Mike: "step back and audit everything we've done and use ChatGPT too ... something is constructed wrong". There are two
independent audits:
- this one (Claude: failure-by-failure on the dev3 build half, which may be read in full, plus check-half diagnosis by class);
- Codex / ChatGPT, read-only: `docs/agere/reviews/2026-10-25-codex-audit.md`.

They agree on the diagnosis.

## What was measured before the fixes

| | dev3 check (71) | held-out (50, once) | Opus competitor, held-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| overall | 60.6 % | 46 % | 50 % (36 % strict) |
| population quality | 10/12 | 20 % | 10 % |
| insight | 4/13 | 10 % | 10 % |
| notes | 4/12 | 60 % | 80 % (quotes not required) |

On held-out, the LLM layers produced most of the wrong answers. When the backstop answered it was right 4 times and wrong 8; the Haiku
analyst was right 1 and wrong 6.

## Root causes (ranked)

1. **Undeclared definitions, not reasoning.** Every one of the 7 build-half insight misses is a defensible analyst answer that uses a
   different definition from the key. Examples:
   - preventable stays 97 / 490 vs key 87 / 391: observation counted or not; the key read visit_group, against decision-context rule 4;
   - transport no-shows 12.3 % vs 9.6 %: the denominator;
   - inpatient claim $11,223 vs $12,343: net vs paid;
   - rising-risk cohort 98–107 vs 125: whether observation counts as an admission;
   - pharmacy spend $3.81M vs $3.12M: claim transactions vs counted fills.

   The keys demand exact numbers. That is why the Opus competitor also scores 10 % on held-out insight and population: no LLM can guess a
   definition nobody wrote down. **Fixed tonight:** Mike decided the four contested definitions (decision-context/3, in
   `config/agere/definition_decisions_2026-10-25.json`):
   - event counts include everyone;
   - observation stays are hospital stays and admissions;
   - pharmacy spend is net of reversals over all claims;
   - medical spend is paid on counted claims.

   The LLM prompts follow them now. Codex is moving the deterministic engines onto them (branch `codex/definitions-2026-10-25`), and the
   dev3 keys are being versioned to them (keys v2, branch `claude/scorer-v2`; v1 kept).
2. **The notes path was not the MGB design.** Check-half examples:
   - evidence retrieval answered "2 passages." and was accepted (x2-06, x2-08);
   - a pattern reader bound "reported status: using" to the wrong medication line (x2-09);
   - a yes/no question was compiled into a count, "0 — self harm thoughts visits" (x2-15);
   - the backstop read a report's header and missed the BI-RADS impression at its end (x2-04).

   MGB (arXiv 2610.27099): the LLM plans an atomic typed question, System One answers it per document, code aggregates and the LLM cites.
   **Fixed:** the backstop has `judge_documents`, in which Jev and then Perplexity judge each WHOLE document (chunked, not one 700-character
   snippet) and code aggregates. It also reads long documents to their end (offsets). Any one-patient question not answered with a quote by
   the certified chart reader is owned by this investigation, so retrieval and pattern layers no longer have the last word.
3. **The cascade was a tournament** (Codex #1). The first proposal that passes shallow checks wins, and the strong model sees the question
   last. **Fixed in part:**
   - the backstop runs before the generalist analysts whatever the learned routers say;
   - it gets its own time, not a 30 s share;
   - it owns one-patient chart questions;
   - the legacy whole-question planners stay off by default.

   The remaining step (Codex #1/#2) is one Haiku→Sonnet investigation flow, with typed metric execution replacing the nested
   natural-language `ask_agere`.
4. **A saved cohort turned the LLM off** (Codex). The backstop and the analyst were not in `TYPED_WITH_POPULATION`, so "how many of them
   ..." follow-ups could never reach an LLM. **Fixed:** the backstop takes the cohort as a `cohort(patient_id)` view.
5. **Provenance bug in today's backstop** (Codex reproduced it): a quote returned for one record validated a citation to another, and a
   keyword search attached "supporting" sentences after the fact. **Fixed:** a quote must be in the cited record's own text or in rows of
   that same record. Keyword attachment is removed; only a System One judgment's passage may be attached.
6. **The scorer is unreliable both ways** (Codex reproduced three false positives: a contradicting quote counts as support, tables are
   matched without column binding, and a rubric passes "Saying they fell is incorrect"). The check half has also been consulted four times.
   **In progress:** scorer v2 plus keys v2 (branch `claude/scorer-v2`). A fresh, independently written evaluation set is the next step
   after that.

## Why MGB's 92 % does not transfer directly

The hosted Jev numbers (LongHealth 91.9 %, TCGA 97.6 %, MedAgentBench 99.4 %) are per-patient, closed-choice chart questions with one
right answer in the text. Agere's notes bucket is that task, and it now uses that design. Population, claims and insight questions are
relational computations whose answers depend on definitions. Those need governed SQL and engines under one plan, with the definitions
decided (cause 1).
