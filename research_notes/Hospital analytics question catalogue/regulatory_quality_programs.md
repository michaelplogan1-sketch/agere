## Q1. Which measures are in each CMS hospital program (IQR, OQR, HRRP, HACRP, VBP, Star Rating, Promoting Interoperability, REH/CAH, price transparency) for current and next program years?

### Takeaway
The CMS hospital measure set in Nov 2026 has four main parts:
- **Claims-based outcomes**, computed by CMS. These are mortality, readmission, EDAC, complication, PSI 90 and MSPB. Most have moved to Medicare Advantage plus FFS cohorts over 2-year periods.
- **NHSN HAIs.**
- **HCAHPS.**
- **A fast-growing hospital-computed eCQM set.** IQR goes from 8 eCQMs (CY2026 reporting) to 9 (CY2027) to 14 (CY2028) to 15 (CY2030). All Hospital Harm eCQMs become mandatory, and mandatory eCQMs feed the Star Rating from their second year.

FY2026–FY2027 rulemaking made three other major changes:
- It removed all health-equity/SDOH structural and screening measures and the HCP COVID-19 vaccination measure across IQR, OQR and REHQR.
- It added sepsis to HRRP, with payment impact from FY2030.
- It replaced OQR's chart-abstracted ED timing measures with the Emergency Care Access & Timeliness eCQM.

### Cited Findings

#### 1A. Hospital IQR (pay-for-reporting; failure = one-fourth reduction of the annual payment update)
- IQR is pay-for-reporting. Hospitals that don't submit data or meet requirements get a one-fourth reduction to their APU (42 CFR 412.140). About 100 hospitals a year fail. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **FY2026 rule removals:** Hospital Commitment to Health Equity, COVID-19 Vaccination Coverage among HCP, Screening for SDOH, and Screen Positive Rate for SDOH. All are removed from the CY2024 reporting period/FY2026 payment determination onward. — [CMS/QRC FY26 summary](https://qualityreportingcenter.com/globalassets/2026/01/iqr/04.-hiqr_summary-of-fy-2026-final-rule-program-changes_vfinal_508.pdf)
- **FY2026 rule modifications:**
  - MORT-30-STK and COMP-HIP-KNEE add MA patients and shorten the period from 3 to 2 years. Stroke mortality for FY2027 uses 7/1/2023–6/30/2025.
  - Risk adjustment for both moves from HCCs to individual ICD-10 codes.
  - The COVID-19 POA exclusion is removed from 2027 for MORT-STK, COMP-HIP-KNEE, AMI/HF/PN EDAC, Hybrid HWR and Hybrid HWM.
  - COMP-HIP-KNEE leaves IQR from FY2030 and enters VBP (modified version) from FY2033. — [CMS/QRC FY26 summary](https://qualityreportingcenter.com/globalassets/2026/01/iqr/04.-hiqr_summary-of-fy-2026-final-rule-program-changes_vfinal_508.pdf)
- **Hybrid measures (FY2026 rule):** From FY2028, Hybrid HWR and HWM require core clinical data elements (CCDE) and linking variables for at least 70% of discharges. Up to 2 missing labs and 2 missing vital signs are allowed. Falling short counts as non-compliance and triggers the one-fourth APU cut. — [CMS/QRC FY26 summary](https://qualityreportingcenter.com/globalassets/2026/01/iqr/04.-hiqr_summary-of-fy-2026-final-rule-program-changes_vfinal_508.pdf)
- **FY2027 rule additions:**
  - Diabetes EDAC, from FY2029 (performance period 7/1/2025–6/30/2027; public July 2028).
  - Hospital Harm–Postoperative VTE eCQM (CBE #5325e). Self-select from CY2028/FY2030, mandatory from CY2030/FY2032.
  - Advance Care Planning eCQM, self-select from CY2028/FY2030.
  - Modified MORT-30 AMI/HF/PN/COPD/CABG, which add MA and use 2-year periods. These return to IQR for FY2028–FY2031 (first period 7/1/2024–6/30/2026; Compare from July 2027) and then move to VBP in FY2032. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **FY2027 rule removals:** VTE-1, VTE-2 and STK-02 eCQMs, from CY2028/FY2030. They are also removed from Promoting Interoperability. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf). Some secondary summaries of the proposed rule misreported STK-02 as a "new" measure, e.g. the [ACDIS/aggregator summaries](https://acdis.org/node/70245). The final-rule summary shows it is a removal.
- **FY2027 rule modifications:**
  - AMI/HF/PN EDAC add MA and use 2-year periods from FY2028 (7/1/2024–6/30/2026), with ICD-10 risk adjustment.
  - Malnutrition Care Score becomes mandatory in CY2028.
  - HH-Falls with Injury and HH-Postoperative Respiratory Failure become mandatory in CY2028.
  - Policy: any new Hospital Harm eCQM becomes mandatory after 2 self-select years. Its first mandatory year is published on the Provider Data Catalog, and from the second year it appears on Care Compare and in the Star Rating.
  - Maternal Morbidity structural measure: from CY2026/FY2028, a "yes" must also name the perinatal QI collaborative. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **IQR measure set by payment year** (HFMA reproduction of final Table IX.C.5). Every column includes these:
  - SEP-1 (CBE #0500, chart-abstracted)
  - NHSN: HCP Influenza Vaccination (#0431), CAUTI (#0138), CLABSI (#0139)
  - MORT-30-STK
  - AMI/HF/PN Excess Days
  - Hybrid HWR (#2879e) and Hybrid HWM (#3502e)
  - Inpatient Surgical Complications Mortality Rate (30-day risk-standardized death rate among surgical inpatients with complications, CBE #4125)
  - HCAHPS (#0166)
  - THA/TKA PRO-PM (#3559)
  - Structural: Maternal Morbidity, Age Friendly Hospital, Patient Safety

  Year-specific items: COMP-HIP-KNEE (FY2028–29 only), the five MORT-30 measures (adopted FY2028, reported FY2029–31) and Diabetes EDAC (FY2030–31 columns). — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **IQR eCQM requirements.** All rows report 4 calendar quarters, with submission due 2 months after the calendar year ends. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)

- **Advance Care Planning eCQM definition:**
  - Denominator: patients ≥18 discharged from an inpatient stay in the CY.
  - Numerator: health-care agent/proxy, advance directive, POLST/MOLST/DNR, or a documented ACP discussion with a decision by discharge on at least one encounter.
  - Exclusions: none. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **HH-Postoperative VTE definition:**
  - Denominator: adults with an OR procedure during the inpatient stay.
  - Numerator: VTE within 30 days of the first procedure.
  - Exclusions: obstetric diagnosis, VTE POA, acute brain/spinal injury POA, ECMO, thrombectomy before or on the day of first surgery, intracranial/spinal surgery with discharge in under 5 days, and stays under 2 days.
  - Risk adjustment: age, sex and 8 clinical factors.
  - Data needed: imaging for VTE plus anticoagulant start within 24 h of that imaging, in the same EHR.
  - The PRMR committee voted 35% to recommend. CMS adopted it anyway and calls it a possible PSI 12 replacement. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Diabetes EDAC definition:**
  - Numerator: days in acute care (ED visit = 1 day, observation hours rounded, unplanned readmissions) within 30 days.
  - Denominator: FFS+MA patients ≥65 with a principal diagnosis of diabetes and 12 months of prior enrollment, discharged alive and not transferred.
  - Exclusions: under 30 days of post-discharge enrollment, AMA discharges, and repeat diabetes index admissions within 30 days.
  - Score: predicted minus expected days per 100 discharges. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Birthing-Friendly Hospital Designation** (Care Compare icon) currently requires a "yes" on the Maternal Morbidity structural measure. In an RFI, CMS floated a 1–3 icon tier: a composite of Cesarean Birth (45%) and Severe Obstetric Complications (55%), with delivery-volume peer groups and k-means clusters. Nothing is finalized. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **FY2027 RFIs, not adopted:**
  - Emergency Care Access & Timeliness eCQM for IQR/VBP.
  - Adult Community-Onset Sepsis Standardized Mortality Ratio, a FHIR dQM: in-hospital death or discharge to hospice. Risk adjustment uses vitals, labs, positive blood cultures, COVID tests, BMI and infection source. PRMR voted 81% for IQR and 57% for VBP. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Age Friendly Hospital measure:** 5 domains with 10 attestation statements, 1 point per domain, for patients ≥65. Domains: eliciting patient goals; responsible medication management; frailty screening/intervention; patient vulnerability (social vulnerability, elder abuse); age-friendly care leadership. The first reporting year was CY2025, attested by 15 May 2026. — [Medisolv (vendor)](https://blog.medisolv.com/articles/cms-age-friendly-structural-measure). Sources conflict on whether a negative attestation affects payment. My background understanding is that full APU requires reporting, not a "yes". **[background, not re-verified]**
- **Patient Safety Structural Measure:** 5 attested domains. Leadership commitment to eliminating preventable harm; strategic planning and organizational policy; culture of safety and learning health system; accountability and transparency; patient and family engagement. — **[background, not re-verified]** [QualityNet IQR measures](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/inpatient/iqr/measures)
- **SEP-1 (CBE #0500):** chart-abstracted, on a sample, reported quarterly. All-or-none bundle for adults with severe sepsis or septic shock:
  - Within 3 h: lactate, blood cultures before antibiotics, broad-spectrum antibiotics.
  - Within 6 h: repeat lactate if the initial value was >2.
  - For septic shock: 30 mL/kg crystalloid, vasopressors for persistent hypotension, and volume/perfusion reassessment.
  - Exclusions include transfer-in, comfort care, and clinical trial participants.

  — **[background, not re-verified]** [QualityNet specifications manual](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/inpatient/specifications-manuals). SEP-1 is also in the VBP Safety domain for FY2027–FY2032 (verified below).

#### 1B. Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP; penalty up to 3% of base operating DRG payments)
- **Conditions for FY2027:** AMI, COPD, HF, pneumonia, CABG and elective THA/TKA. Each uses a 30-day risk-standardized unplanned readmission measure turned into an excess readmission ratio (ERR).
- **Method:**
  - Each hospital's weighted ERR is compared with the median of its peer group. Peer groups are quintiles by the share of full-benefit dual-eligible patients.
  - Payment factor ranges from 1.0 to 0.97.
  - FY2027 applicable period is 1/1/2022–12/31/2023, and hospitals need at least 25 cases on at least one measure.
  - 2,358 of 2,832 hospitals (83%) are penalized, with penalties averaging 0.48% of payments. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Sepsis Readmission measure added (FY2027 final):** confidential "early look" reports for FY2028 (7/1/2024–6/30/2026) and FY2029, then payment and public reporting from FY2030 (7/1/2026–6/30/2028).
  - Cohort: FFS+MA patients ≥65 with a principal diagnosis of sepsis, including post-procedural sepsis.
  - Exclusions: AMA discharges, under 30 days of post-discharge enrollment, discharge to hospice, sepsis cases already in the pneumonia measure, and repeat sepsis index admissions within 30 days.
  - Risk adjustment includes organism aggressiveness, transplant status and severe-sepsis clinical markers.
  - Data: FFS claims, MA claims and encounter data, using 2 years of claims. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **FY2026 rule:** MA patients added to HRRP claims measures, with 2-year periods. — [CMS/QRC FY26 summary](https://qualityreportingcenter.com/globalassets/2026/01/iqr/04.-hiqr_summary-of-fy-2026-final-rule-program-changes_vfinal_508.pdf); [BDO FY26 summary](https://www.bdo.com/insights/blogs/bdo-knows-healthcare/5-key-updates-in-the-medicare-fy26-ipps-and-ltch-pps-final-rule)

#### 1C. Hospital-Acquired Condition Reduction Program (HACRP; 1% cut to worst-performing quartile)
- **Measures for FY2027 and later:** NHSN CAUTI (#0138), CDI (#1717), CLABSI (#0139), Colon and Abdominal Hysterectomy SSI (#0753), MRSA bacteremia (#1716), and CMS PSI 90 (#0531).
- **Scoring:** a Winsorized z-score per measure. The Total HAC Score is the equally weighted average, and the worst quartile is penalized. 721 of 2,891 hospitals are penalized for FY2027. FY2027 made no changes.
- **Specifications:** CDC NHSN and QualityNet PSI pages. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **PSI 90 components:** PSI 03 pressure ulcer, PSI 06 iatrogenic pneumothorax, PSI 08 in-hospital fall with hip fracture, PSI 09 postoperative hemorrhage/hematoma, PSI 10 postoperative AKI requiring dialysis, PSI 11 postoperative respiratory failure, PSI 12 perioperative PE/DVT, PSI 13 postoperative sepsis, PSI 14 wound dehiscence, PSI 15 abdominopelvic accidental puncture/laceration. CMS computes it from Medicare claims over 24 months (e.g., 7/1/2022–6/30/2024). — [Leapfrog Fall 2026 measures](https://www.hospitalsafetygrade.org/media/file/Safety-Grade-Reporting-Periods-Fall-2026-ANTICIPATED.pdf)
- **NHSN HAI metric:** a standardized infection ratio (SIR = observed / predicted HAIs), using NHSN surveillance definitions, device-days and patient-days denominators, and baseline risk models. Data come from infection-prevention surveillance (lab cultures, device days, procedure lists) entered or uploaded to NHSN monthly. CMS pulls the data quarterly. — **[background, not re-verified]** [CDC NHSN acute care hospital protocols](https://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/acute-care-hospital/index.html)

#### 1D. Hospital Value-Based Purchasing (VBP; 2% withhold redistributed by Total Performance Score)
- **Funding:** 2% of base operating DRG payments goes into the pool, about $1.9B for FY2027. Across 2,448 hospitals, the average net adjustment is +0.132%, with 51.7% positive. A Table 16B with actual factors is due on the CMS website in fall 2026. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Domains and measures (FY2027; FY2028–2032):**
  - **Clinical Outcomes:** MORT-30 AMI (#0230), HF (#0229), PN (#0468), COPD (#1893), CABG (#2558), and COMP-HIP-KNEE (#1550).
  - **Safety:** NHSN CLABSI, CAUTI, Colon/Hysterectomy SSI, MRSA, CDI, and SEP-1 (#0500).
  - **Person and Community Engagement:** HCAHPS. Dimensions are Nurse communication, Doctor communication, Responsiveness*, Communication about medicines, Discharge information, Care transition*, Cleanliness/quietness, and Overall rating. *Responsiveness and Care Transition are unscored FY2027–2029 and dropped from FY2030, when the updated HCAHPS is used. During FY2027–2029 the base score is 6 dimensions × 10 points, rescaled by 8/6, plus up to 20 consistency points.
  - **Efficiency and Cost Reduction:** MSPB (#2158). — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Performance periods (FY2028 program year):**
  - HCAHPS, NHSN, SEP-1 and MSPB: CY2026 (baseline CY2024).
  - Mortality: 7/1/2023–6/30/2026.
  - COMP-HIP-KNEE: 4/1/2023–3/31/2026.
  - FY2032 mortality uses the modified 2-year FFS+MA version (7/1/2028–6/30/2030).
  - COMP-HIP-KNEE modified version from FY2033. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Domain weights:** each of the four domains is weighted 25%. — **[background, not re-verified]** [QualityNet HVBP](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/inpatient/hvbp)

#### 1E. Overall Hospital Quality Star Rating (public; annual)
- **Method:**
  - Five measure groups: Safety of Care, Mortality, Readmission and Patient Experience at 22% each; Timely and Effective Care at 12%.
  - Scores are standardized and averaged within each group.
  - Hospitals need at least 3 measures in at least 3 groups, one of which must be Mortality or Safety.
  - Peer groups are 3, 4 or 5 groups, and stars are assigned by k-means.
- **CY2026 OPPS final change:**
  - 2026 rating: a 4-star cap for hospitals in the lowest Safety of Care quartile (needs at least 3 safety measures). This affected 14 of 2,847 hospitals in simulation.
  - 2027 rating and later: a 1-star reduction for the lowest Safety quartile, affecting 459 of 2,847 in simulation. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **Hospital Harm eCQMs** enter Care Compare and the Star Rating from their second mandatory year. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)

#### 1F. Hospital OQR (pay-for-reporting; failure = 2.0 percentage-point OPD update cut)
- **Penalty:** reporting ratio 0.9805 for CY2026. A secondary source reports 0.9804 for CY2027 [HK Law](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/11/cms-issues-sweeping-cy-2027-hospital-opps-asc-final-rule). 42 hospitals failed and 54 opted out for the 2025 payment determination. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **Measure set by payment determination 2026–2031** (HFMA reproduction of Table 138):
  - **Claims-based:** Abdomen CT Use of Contrast; Facility 7-Day Hospital Visits after Outpatient Colonoscopy (#2539); Admissions and ED Visits for Outpatient Chemotherapy (#3490); Hospital Visits within 7 Days after Outpatient Surgery (#2687); Breast Cancer Screening Recall Rates.
  - **Chart-abstracted:** Median Time ED Arrival to Departure for Discharged Patients and Left Without Being Seen, both through PD2029 and removed from PD2030; Head CT/MRI interpretation within 45 min for stroke (#0661); Appropriate Follow-Up Interval for Normal Colonoscopy (#0658).
  - **eCQMs:** STEMI eCQM (mandatory); Emergency Care Access & Timeliness (#4625e), voluntary CY2027 and mandatory CY2028/PD2030; Excessive Radiation CT (#3663e), voluntary indefinitely after mandatory reporting was reversed.
  - **Surveys:** OAS CAHPS, mandatory from PD2027; Cataract visual function survey (voluntary).
  - **PRO-PMs:** THA/TKA PRO-PM in the HOPD, mandatory PD2031; Information Transfer PRO-PM (#4210), mandatory PD2029.
  - **Removed:** HCP COVID vaccination (from 2024), HCHE, SDOH screening and Screen Positive (from 2025). — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **Emergency Care Access & Timeliness eCQM:**
  - Denominator: all ED encounters, all ages and payers, over 12 months.
  - Numerator: any of four events. (1) Arrival to treatment space over 1 h. (2) Left without being evaluated. (3) Boarding from decision-to-admit order to ED departure over 4 h. (4) ED LOS from arrival to physical departure over 8 h.
  - ED-observation encounters are excluded from components 3 and 4.
  - Score: volume-adjusted z-score; multi-ED CCNs are weighted.
  - Strata: age ≥18 vs <18, and with vs without mental-health diagnosis.
  - Mandatory years require 4 quarters, submitted by 15 May. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **CY2027 OPPS final** (issued 4 Nov 2026, published in the Federal Register 6 Nov 2026):
  - Removes Appropriate Follow-Up Interval for Normal Colonoscopy from OQR and ASCQR, from CY2027 reporting/CY2029 PD.
  - Updates OQR data validation, including eCQM validation.
  - No REHQR changes. — [CMS CY27 OPPS fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-2027-hospital-outpatient-prospective-payment-system-opps-ambulatory-surgical-center-0); [BakerHostetler](https://www.bakerlaw.com/insights/cms-releases-the-2027-hospital-outpatient-prospective-payment-system-and-ambulatory-surgical-center-final-rule/)

#### 1G. Rural Emergency Hospitals (REHQR) and Critical Access Hospitals
- **REHQR:** statute requires at least quarterly submission and public posting, with no payment incentive or penalty. 38 REHs as of 2025.
  - Measures: Abdomen CT contrast, 7-day visits after colonoscopy, and 7-day visits after outpatient surgery (claims).
  - Median Time for Discharged ED Patients, OR the REH version of the Emergency Care Access & Timeliness eCQM from CY2027/PD2029. The REH version counts ED boarding before transfer over 4 h.
  - HCHE and SDOH measures removed. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf); [CMS CY27 fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-2027-hospital-outpatient-prospective-payment-system-opps-ambulatory-surgical-center-0)
- **CAHs in Medicare Promoting Interoperability:** a CAH that isn't a meaningful EHR user is paid 100% instead of 101% of reasonable cost. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **CAHs in MBQIP:** submitting MBQIP data is the only quality requirement for Flex participation, covering about 1,350 CAHs. The updated core set from CY2025 has core and additional measures across 5 domains: global, patient safety, patient engagement, care coordination and ED. Annual CY2025 data were due about 27–28 Feb 2026, and ED measures such as OP-18 are quarterly. — [Telligen MBQIP](https://telligen.com/rqita/mbqip-measures); [Flex Monitoring/Telligen deadlines](https://www.telligen.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/MonthlyMBQIPDataSubmissionDeadlines_November2025.pdf). I did not open the measure list itself (see Gaps).

#### 1H. Medicare Promoting Interoperability (eligible hospitals and CAHs)
- **Penalty:** IPPS hospitals that aren't meaningful users lose three-quarters of the market basket. Passing requires at least 80 points (EHR reporting periods from 2026).
- **Scoring for 2027:**
  - e-Prescribing (10) and Query of PDMP (10).
  - Health Information Exchange (30), via one of three routes: the two Support Electronic Referral Loops measures (15+15), HIE Bi-Directional Exchange (30), or Enabling Exchange under TEFCA (30).
  - Electronic Prior Authorization: 10 bonus points, optional in 2027; required but unscored from 2028.
  - Provide Patients Electronic Access (25).
  - Public Health (25): Syndromic Surveillance, Immunization Registry, Electronic Case Reporting, Electronic Lab Reporting, Antimicrobial Use Surveillance, Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance, plus the new Unique Device Identifiers for Implantable Devices measure from 2027. Bonus 5 for one of Public Health Registry, Clinical Data Registry or Public Health Reporting via TEFCA.
  - Required but unscored: Security Risk Analysis, SAFER Guides, information-blocking attestation and eCQM reporting.
  - The two Referral Loops measures are removed from 2029.
  - ONC Direct Review and ONC-ACB attestations are removed from the 2026 reporting period.
  - The CEHRT definition is revised for HTI-5. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)

### Inferences
- **The growth is in eCQMs the hospital computes itself.** The operational burden is shifting from chart abstraction to certified-EHR logic plus QRDA I files. By CY2028, 11 eCQMs are mandatory. Seven of them are Hospital Harm or outcome measures that need medication administration (opioid/naloxone, insulin/dextrose), point-of-care and lab glucose, creatinine, falls documentation, pressure-injury staging, ventilation timestamps, and nutrition screening/assessment documentation.
- **Medicare Advantage now matters for hospital-side replication.** MA is folding into every claims-based outcome: mortality, EDAC, readmissions, COMP-HIP-KNEE and the sepsis readmission measure. A hospital trying to replicate CMS results internally needs payer-agnostic encounter data. FFS claims alone no longer reproduce CMS's cohort.
- **ED throughput changes from medians to a threshold composite.** Under OQR the composite is pay-for-reporting, but it will be public, and CMS is considering it for IQR/VBP. Timestamp data quality will become a public-reporting issue: arrival, roomed, decision-to-admit order, departure and LWBS disposition.

## Q4. Which measures can be computed from a hospital's own warehouse, and which come from CMS claims calculation or vendor surveys?

### Takeaway
Hospitals can fully compute and submit the eCQMs, SEP-1, chart-abstracted OQR measures, the EHR half of the hybrid measures, NHSN HAI SIR inputs, structural attestations, the Leapfrog survey inputs, price-transparency allowed amounts and state registries. CMS calculates the claims-based outcomes (MA encounters included): mortality, readmissions, EDAC, COMP, PSI 90, MSPB, and the OQR/REH claims measures. Hospitals can approximate these internally, but they can't reproduce CMS's cross-hospital readmissions and risk models exactly. HCAHPS and OAS CAHPS come from certified survey vendors. PRO-PMs need hospital-collected surveys linked to CMS claims.

### Cited Findings
- **eCQMs** are "calculated by the hospital's certified health IT using patient-level data" and submitted as QRDA I files, with abstraction from non-certified sources permitted. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Mortality and EDAC** are calculated by CMS from FFS claims, hospital-submitted MA claims, MA organization encounter data and the Medicare Enrollment Database. They are publicly reported annually on rolling 24-month data. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **Readmissions:** the HRRP Sepsis Readmission measure uses FFS Part A/B claims plus MA claims and encounters, with hierarchical logistic regression (predicted/expected). — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
- **ED timing:** the Emergency Care Access & Timeliness eCQM is extracted electronically from the EHR and replaces chart-abstracted ED measures. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **Price transparency:** allowed amounts must be computed from the hospital's own 835 remittance data. — [HFMA CY26 OPPS final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/510110_CY_2026_OPPS_ASC_Final_Rule_Summary.pdf)
- **Leapfrog:** HAIs come from the Leapfrog Survey with CMS as fallback, and PSIs from CMS. — [Leapfrog Fall 2026](https://www.hospitalsafetygrade.org/media/file/Safety-Grade-Reporting-Periods-Fall-2026-ANTICIPATED.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Time lags differ by class.** Class B results lag 1–3 years (FY2027 HRRP uses 2022–2023 discharges; Leapfrog PSIs use 7/2022–6/2024). Internal analytics should mirror CMS logic on current data to flag likely penalties years ahead. Payer-agnostic encounter data and the hospital's own 30-day return visits capture only part of the readmissions and EDAC numerator.
- **Validation needs a known-good reference.** Many measures have a published reference value (Care Compare/PDC files, hospital-specific reports, QualityNet feedback reports). An internal calculation can be validated against these.

### Gaps
- No primary source quantifies how closely internal hospital replication matches CMS readmission or mortality results. The share of post-discharge events seen at the index hospital is cited only as about 75% of surgical-complication returns going back to the discharging hospital, in the VTE discussion. — [HFMA FY27 final](https://www.hfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/510155-FY-2027-IPPS_LTCH-PPS-Final-Rule-Summary.pdf)
