The most important unfinished work, in order:

1. **Reliable clinical meaning and patient state.** Broaden structured/narrative interpretation, bind unresolved questions to exact evidence, handle dates/copies/conflicts and distinguish current medication use, intent, tolerance, monitoring and completed care. Add independent reviewed reference examples and calibrated deferral. Preserve and investigate the known respiratory identity hypothesis and functional Boolean-field ambiguity.
2. **Clinical and payer applicability.** Add reviewed, versioned clinical knowledge and authentic CMS/commercial/Medicaid plan and contract criteria. Combine indication, patient goals, consent, network participation, authorization, effective dates and actual buyer services. Generic program knowledge is insufficient for patient-specific eligibility.
3. **Useful predictions, patterns and recommendations.** Establish defensible labels/follow-up, compare simple baselines, quantify calibration and uncertainty, and test whether recommendations improve decisions. Timing-safe prediction experiments currently lack adequate targets; do not force fits or claim outcome benefits.
4. **Real care-system execution.** Implement customer-approved EHR, pharmacy, referral, scheduling and team adapters with verified roles, operational capacity, consent, acknowledgment, completion and outcome evidence. These are not live today.
5. **Complete VBC and financial workflows.** Finish opportunity-to-program-to-measure-to-contract/payment links, authentic attribution/formulas, case-mix adjustment, benchmarks, program cost and settlement reconciliation. Keep opportunity separate from realized savings.
6. **Operational durability and production packaging.** Add audited stale-answer retirement/supersession and preservation of other optional contexts; finish customer schema portability, continuous ingestion, tenant/security controls, reproducible deployment/recovery, latency/usability qualification and a broad end-to-end acceptance curriculum.
